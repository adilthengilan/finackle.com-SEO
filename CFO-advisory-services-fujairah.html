UAE tax context

UAE tax rules your
CFO plan should reflect.

Good forecasting includes the cash needed for tax, not just operating costs.

Corporate tax (9%)

UAE corporate tax applies at 9% on taxable income above AED 375,000. A CFO forecasts the cash needed for the liability and keeps management reports aligned with your accountant's filings. Federal Tax Authority corporate tax guidance.

VAT (5%)

The standard UAE VAT rate is 5%. Cash flow forecasts should include VAT payable and refunds so payment dates do not catch you out. Federal Tax Authority VAT guidance.

Free zone status

Qualifying Free Zone Persons can access a 0% rate on qualifying income when conditions are met. Budgets and reports should separate qualifying from non-qualifying income.

Working with your accountant

Finackle's CFO support covers planning, forecasting and reporting. It works alongside your accountant or registered tax agent, who handle statutory filings.
