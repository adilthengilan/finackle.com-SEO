The challenge

Growing businesses rarely lack data. They lack clarity.

Financial information is fragmented, processes are manual, and there is limited visibility into profitability, cash flow and performance. Without reliable reporting and structured systems, confident decisions get harder.

Fragmented information

Sales in one tool, bank in another, receipts in a chat thread. No single version of the truth.

Which spreadsheet is the right one?

Manual processes

Copy-paste reconciliations, chasing approvals, rebuilding the same report every month.

Month-end takes us three weeks.

Limited visibility

Profit by product, next month’s cash, the real cost of a channel. Too often it’s a guess.

Profitable on paper, short on cash.

                      FAQ

                      Questions before we talk

                      How is this different from a bookkeeper?+

                      A bookkeeper records transactions. We design and run your finance function: the structure, controls, reporting and compliance calendar, so the numbers stay reliable and useful as you grow.

                      Do you work with free zone and mainland companies?+

                      Yes. We support businesses across UAE free zones and the mainland, and shape reporting and compliance around your structure.

                      What does the Free Finance Health Check include?+

                      Two parts, both free. The 10-question Health Check gives you a personalised Finance Health Report straight away: your score, key risks, top priorities and a 90-day plan. If you like, we then go through it with you in a 30-minute Finance Health Review call and send a written summary. There is no obligation to continue.

                      Our books are behind. Can you still help?+

                      Yes. Clean-up is often where we start. We scope it with you and agree it before any work begins.

                      Which accounting software do you use?+

                      We work with leading cloud accounting systems and can build around what you already use. If you don’t have one yet, we’ll recommend the right fit for your size.

                      How do you keep our data secure?+

                      Access is limited to your assigned team, we use view-only bank access where possible, and your information is never shared without your permission.
