What does a UAE e-commerce accountant do?

A UAE e-commerce accountant keeps your books in line with how online selling really works here: COD orders settled weeks later by couriers, marketplace payouts net of fees, gateway settlements, returns, and VAT on every sale. The result is books you can trust for VAT returns, corporate tax and day-to-day decisions.

Where UAE online sellers lose track

Four places the money
goes missing.

Most accounting gaps in UAE online businesses appear in the same few places. We build the books around them.

01

COD cash arrives late and short

Couriers remit in batches, net of fees and returns. Without order-level matching, you cannot tell what is still owed to you.

02

Marketplace payouts hide the real fees

Commissions, fulfilment, ads and refunds are netted off before payout, so bank receipts never equal sales.

03

VAT and corporate tax need clean records

5% VAT and 9% corporate tax both depend on correct revenue, expense and stock records.

04

Stock value is guesswork

Imports, landed costs and returned goods distort margins unless inventory is costed properly.

FAQ

UAE e-commerce accountant: your questions

How do you account for cash on delivery (COD) orders?

We record COD sales when delivered, then match courier remittances to individual orders, so fees, returns and unremitted cash are visible instead of hidden in one bank deposit.

Is corporate tax relevant to my online store?

UAE corporate tax is 9% on taxable income above AED 375,000 for most businesses. Clean, reconciled books make it easier to prepare the figures your tax adviser needs. Confirm your own position with the Federal Tax Authority or a tax adviser.

Do you work with mainland and free zone companies?

Yes, we support online businesses in all emirates, whether licensed on the mainland or in a free zone. VAT and tax treatment can differ, so we review each setup individually.

Which platforms can you reconcile?

Own websites (Shopify, WooCommerce), marketplaces such as Amazon.ae and Noon, payment gateways, social-commerce sales and courier COD remittances.

Can you take over messy books from a previous accountant?

Yes. We start with a clean-up review of past transactions, bank statements and VAT records, then move you onto a monthly routine.
