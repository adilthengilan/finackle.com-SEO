Where UAE online sellers lose track

Four places the money
goes missing.

Most accounting gaps in UAE online businesses appear in the same few places. We build the books around them.

01

COD cash arrives late and short

Couriers remit in batches, net of fees and returns. Without order-level matching, you cannot tell what is still owed to you.

02

Marketplace payouts hide the real fees

Commissions, fulfilment, ads and refunds are netted off before payout, so bank receipts never equal sales.

03

VAT and corporate tax need clean records

5% VAT and 9% corporate tax both depend on correct revenue, expense and stock records.

04

Stock value is guesswork

Imports, landed costs and returned goods distort margins unless inventory is costed properly.

UAE tax for online sellers

VAT and corporate tax
quick facts.

A short reference for UAE online sellers. Rules can change, so always confirm current requirements with the Federal Tax Authority.

UAE VAT and corporate tax essentials
VAT rate5% on most taxable supplies in the UAE.
VAT registrationMandatory above AED 375,000 of taxable supplies and imports in 12 months; voluntary above AED 187,500.
Corporate tax9% on taxable income above AED 375,000 for most businesses. Your own position may differ.
Records to keepTax invoices, credit notes, import documents, courier remittance reports, marketplace and gateway settlements, bank statements.
Why e-commerce is differentCOD timing, netted marketplace payouts and returns mean bank deposits never equal sales, so each channel must reconcile to your VAT and income records.

Source: UAE Federal Tax Authority. General information only, not tax advice.

UAE e-commerce accountant: your questions

What does a UAE e-commerce accountant do?

A UAE e-commerce accountant keeps your books in line with how online selling works here: COD orders settled by couriers weeks later, marketplace payouts net of fees, gateway settlements, returns and VAT on every sale, so your records support VAT returns, corporate tax and daily decisions.

How do you account for cash on delivery (COD) orders?

We record COD sales when delivered, then match courier remittances to individual orders, so fees, returns and unremitted cash are visible instead of hidden in one bank deposit.

Does my online store need to register for VAT in the UAE?

UAE VAT is 5%. Registration is mandatory when taxable supplies and imports exceed AED 375,000 in 12 months, and voluntary above AED 187,500. Confirm current rules with the Federal Tax Authority.

Is corporate tax relevant to my online store?

UAE corporate tax is 9% on taxable income above AED 375,000 for most businesses. Clean, reconciled books make it easier to prepare the figures your tax adviser needs. Confirm your own position with the Federal Tax Authority or a tax adviser.

Do you work with mainland and free zone companies?

Yes, we support online businesses in all emirates, whether licensed on the mainland or in a free zone. VAT and tax treatment can differ, so we review each setup individually. See also our pages for Dubai and Abu Dhabi.

Which platforms can you reconcile?

Own websites (Shopify, WooCommerce), marketplaces such as Amazon.ae and Noon, payment gateways, social-commerce sales and courier COD remittances.

Can you take over messy books from a previous accountant?

Yes. We start with a clean-up review of past transactions, bank statements and VAT records, then move you onto a monthly routine.

How much does an e-commerce accountant cost in the UAE?

Cost depends on monthly order volume, number of channels and couriers, inventory complexity and whether VAT work is included. After a short review we recommend a scope and quote.
