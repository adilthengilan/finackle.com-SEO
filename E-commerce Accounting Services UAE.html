UAE VAT for online sellers

UAE VAT quick facts
for e-commerce.

A short reference for online sellers. Rules can change, so confirm current requirements with the UAE Federal Tax Authority. General information, not tax advice.

UAE VAT essentials for e-commerce
Standard VAT rate5% on most taxable supplies in the UAE.
Mandatory registrationWhen taxable supplies and imports exceed AED 375,000 in the previous 12 months or are expected to in the next 30 days.
Voluntary registrationAvailable when taxable supplies or expenses exceed AED 187,500.
Records to keepTax invoices, credit notes, import documents, sales and purchase records, and marketplace or gateway settlement statements.
Why e-commerce is differentRefunds, marketplace fees, shipping and multi-channel sales all affect the VAT you charge and can reclaim.

What is e-commerce accounting in the UAE?

E-commerce accounting is bookkeeping and financial reporting designed for online sellers. It matches orders, refunds, marketplace settlements and payouts with bank receipts, and tracks inventory, fees and VAT so businesses in the UAE can see true profit.

Do e-commerce businesses in the UAE need to register for VAT?

UAE VAT is 5%. Registration is mandatory when taxable supplies and imports exceed AED 375,000 in 12 months, and voluntary above AED 187,500. Confirm current rules with the Federal Tax Authority.

Do you serve online sellers in every emirate?

Yes. We support e-commerce businesses across the UAE, including Abu Dhabi, Dubai, Fujairah, Umm Al Quwain and Ras Al Khaimah.

How do you handle cash on delivery?

We reconcile courier remittances to orders and flag collections that are overdue or short.

Do free-zone sellers need different VAT records?

Treatment can differ for designated zones and goods movements, so we keep clear records and recommend confirming specifics with the Federal Tax Authority.

Can you combine accounts for several UAE companies?

Yes. We can produce group-level reports across entities that sell through different channels.
