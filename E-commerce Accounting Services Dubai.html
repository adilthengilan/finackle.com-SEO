UAE VAT for Dubai online sellers

UAE VAT quick facts
for e-commerce.

A short reference for online sellers. Rules can change, so confirm current requirements with the UAE Federal Tax Authority. General information, not tax advice.

UAE VAT essentials for e-commerce
Standard VAT rate5% on most taxable supplies in the UAE.
Mandatory registrationWhen taxable supplies and imports exceed AED 375,000 in the previous 12 months, or are expected to in the next 30 days.
Voluntary registrationAvailable when taxable supplies or expenses exceed AED 187,500.
Records to keepTax invoices, credit notes, import documents, sales and purchase records, and marketplace or gateway settlement statements. UAE VAT rules generally require these to be kept for at least five years.
Why e-commerce is differentRefunds, marketplace fees, shipping and multi-channel sales all affect the VAT you charge and can reclaim.

What is e-commerce accounting in Dubai?

E-commerce accounting in Dubai is bookkeeping and financial reporting designed for online sellers. It matches orders, refunds, marketplace settlements, courier collections and payouts with bank receipts, and tracks inventory, fees and VAT so businesses can see true profit.

Do e-commerce businesses in Dubai need to register for VAT?

UAE VAT is 5%. Registration is mandatory when taxable supplies and imports exceed AED 375,000 in the previous 12 months, or are expected to in the next 30 days. Voluntary registration is available above AED 187,500. Confirm current rules with the Federal Tax Authority.

Can you handle Shopify, Amazon, Noon and WooCommerce sales for Dubai sellers?

Yes. We reconcile sales from websites, marketplaces and payment gateways against refunds, fees and bank receipts.

Do you support Dubai sellers on Amazon and Noon?

Yes. We reconcile Amazon and Noon settlements, fees and refunds against bank receipts for sellers based in Dubai.

Can you handle cash-on-delivery reconciliation in Dubai?

Yes. We match COD collections from couriers against orders and bank deposits, and flag unpaid, returned or short-remitted orders.

How do marketplace fees and refunds affect UAE VAT?

Refunds and credit notes reduce the VAT you charged. Marketplace commissions, fulfilment and shipping fees usually carry VAT that may be recoverable as input VAT if you are VAT-registered and hold valid tax invoices. We record these separately so your VAT reconciliation stays accurate.

Do Dubai free-zone sellers need different VAT records?

Treatment can differ for designated zones and goods movements, so we keep clear records and recommend confirming specifics with the Federal Tax Authority.

Do Dubai online sellers pay corporate tax?

UAE corporate tax applies at 9% on taxable income above AED 375,000, with specific rules for qualifying free zone businesses. Clean books by channel and product make it easier to support your filings. Confirm your position with the Federal Tax Authority or a tax adviser.

How long should e-commerce accounting records be kept in the UAE?

UAE VAT rules generally require tax records such as invoices, credit notes and sales and purchase records to be kept for at least five years. Marketplace and gateway settlement statements should be kept with them. Confirm current requirements with the Federal Tax Authority.

Can you take over from my current bookkeeper or clean up old books?

Yes. We start by reviewing your existing records, sales channels and VAT position, fix reconciliation gaps, and then set up a repeatable monthly routine.

Cost depends on order volume, number of channels, inventory complexity and whether VAT returns are included. After a short review we recommend a scope and quote.
