Restaurant finance basics

The numbers every
owner should know.

Three measures we track for Abu Dhabi restaurants, cafes and cloud kitchens.

Food cost %

The share of food sales spent on the ingredients you sold. A rising food cost % often points to wastage, portioning or supplier price changes.

Food cost % = cost of food sold ÷ food sales × 100

Prime cost

Your two biggest controllable costs together: what you spend on food and beverage plus what you spend on labour. Tracking it as a % of sales shows whether the business is under control.

Prime cost = food and beverage cost + labour cost

Break-even sales

The sales an outlet needs in a month to cover all its costs. It is the key number before opening a new site or changing a lease.

Break-even sales = fixed costs ÷ contribution margin %

What is F&B finance support in Abu Dhabi?

F&B finance support in Abu Dhabi gives restaurants, cafes, cloud kitchens and food businesses finance-team capability without a full-time hire. It covers food cost analysis, cash flow management, budgeting and forecasting, management reporting and profitability by outlet, menu category and sales channel.

Do you support Abu Dhabi cloud kitchens and cafes?

Yes. We support cafes, restaurants, catering firms, hotel outlets and cloud kitchens in Abu Dhabi, whether you operate on the mainland or in a free zone. We set up monthly reporting, food cost tracking and cash planning around your licence type and outlet structure, and flag where your legal structure may affect VAT or corporate tax.

Can you track food cost across several outlets?

Yes. We compare purchases, stock and sales by outlet and menu category, then highlight variances in food cost percentage, wastage and portioning. You see which outlets and dishes drift from target, so you can act on supplier prices, recipes or controls instead of finding the problem months later in the accounts.

Can you plan cash for slow-paying catering clients?

Yes. We forecast expected receipts from catering and corporate clients against supplier, payroll and rent payments, week by week. Gaps show up early, so you can adjust purchasing, chase overdue invoices or arrange funding in time. We also track receivables ageing so slow payers are visible in your monthly report.

Can you report on delivery platform commissions?

Yes. We reconcile delivery-platform sales, commissions, refunds and payouts to your accounts, then show net margin by platform against dine-in. This reveals whether delivery orders really earn a profit after food cost, packaging and commission, and which platforms or menu items are worth promoting.

Do restaurants and food businesses in Abu Dhabi need to register for VAT?

UAE VAT registration is mandatory when taxable supplies and imports exceed AED 375,000 over the previous 12 months or are expected to in the next 30 days. Voluntary registration is possible above AED 187,500. The standard rate is 5%. Check the Federal Tax Authority for current rules.

Does UAE corporate tax apply to restaurants and cafes in Abu Dhabi?

UAE corporate tax is 9% on taxable income above AED 375,000. Whether and how it applies to your business depends on your legal structure and free-zone status, so confirm with the Federal Tax Authority or a registered tax adviser. Good management reporting makes profit and tax planning easier to forecast.
