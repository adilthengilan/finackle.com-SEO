What is F&B finance support in Fujairah?

F&B finance support in Fujairah gives restaurants, cafes, cloud kitchens and food businesses finance-team capability without a full-time hire. It covers food cost analysis, cash flow management, budgeting and forecasting, management reporting and profitability by outlet, menu category and sales channel.

How is F&B finance different from restaurant bookkeeping?

Bookkeeping records what happened. F&B finance support uses those records to analyse food cost, labour and wastage, compare budget with actual results, forecast cash and show which outlets, menu items and channels make money.

Can you help control food cost and margins?

Yes. We compare purchases, stock and sales to show food cost and gross margin by outlet and menu category, and highlight variances so you can act on pricing, portioning, wastage and supplier costs.

Can you track delivery aggregator commissions and payouts?

Yes. We reconcile delivery platform sales, commissions, refunds and payouts against bank receipts, so you can see the true margin on delivery orders compared with dine-in sales.

Do restaurants and food businesses need to register for VAT?

UAE VAT registration is mandatory when taxable supplies and imports exceed AED 375,000 over the previous 12 months or are expected to in the next 30 days. Voluntary registration is possible above AED 187,500. The standard rate is 5%. Check the Federal Tax Authority for current rules.

Can you support multi-outlet restaurants and cloud kitchens in Fujairah?

Yes. We support single-outlet and multi-outlet restaurants, cafes, cloud kitchens and catering businesses in Fujairah, on the mainland or in free zones such as Fujairah Free Zone and Creative City. We report performance by outlet, menu category and sales channel, and review VAT treatment against Federal Tax Authority guidance.
