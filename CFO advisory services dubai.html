Is it time?

When does your Dubai business need CFO support?

Having accurate accounts is essential, but historical financial information does not always help you decide what to do next.

Your Dubai business may benefit from CFO support if:

1

Cash flow is difficult to predict

You need better visibility over customer collections, supplier payments, payroll commitments and upcoming investments.

2

Your business is growing but profitability is unclear

Revenue growth does not always mean better margins. You need to understand which products, services, customers or channels are actually contributing to profit.

3

Management reports do not support decisions

You receive financial statements but need clearer explanations of performance, trends and areas requiring attention.

4

You are planning expansion

Opening a new branch, hiring employees, increasing inventory or launching new services requires understanding the financial impact before committing resources.

5

Your business has outgrown spreadsheets

Reporting takes too much time, information is spread across different systems and management lacks timely visibility.

6

Banks, investors or partners require stronger financial information

You need structured forecasts, financial analysis and reporting to support important discussions.

CFO advisory services

What is included in our CFO advisory services?

Finackle’s CFO support connects accounting information with planning, analysis and business decision-making. The exact scope depends on your business model, existing finance processes and the level of support required.

1

Cash Flow Forecasting and Working Capital Management

Profit does not always equal available cash. Dubai businesses often need to manage customer payment cycles, inventory purchases, supplier commitments and growth investments.

We help develop visibility into

  • Rolling cash flow forecasts, including 13-week forecasts where appropriate
  • Expected customer collections
  • Supplier payment requirements
  • Working capital movements
  • Potential cash shortfalls
  • Impact of planned investments
Outcome: Better understanding of when cash will be available, when pressure may arise and what actions should be considered.
2

Management Reporting and Financial Performance

Financial statements show what happened. Management reporting helps explain why it happened and what decisions may be required.

We help structure management reports including

  • Monthly profit and loss reporting
  • Balance sheet and cash flow analysis
  • Key business KPIs
  • Budget versus actual analysis
  • Margin analysis
  • Management commentary and priorities
Outcome: Less time reviewing numbers and more time acting on insights.
3

Budgeting and Financial Forecasting

A budget sets expectations. Forecasting helps you understand how your business is performing against those expectations.

Support may include

  • Annual budgets
  • Rolling forecasts
  • Revenue assumptions
  • Cost planning
  • Hiring decisions
  • Expansion scenarios
  • Investment analysis
Outcome: A clearer view of what your business plans require financially.
4

Profitability, Pricing and Margin Analysis

Growing sales does not automatically create profitable growth. Factors such as supplier pricing, delivery costs, discounts, platform fees and operating expenses can significantly affect margins.

We help analyse

  • Gross and operating margins
  • Product profitability
  • Service profitability
  • Customer/channel profitability
  • Cost structures
  • Pricing decisions
Outcome: Better understanding of where profit is generated and where improvements may be required.
5

Financial Support for Growth Decisions

Business owners often need financial clarity before making important decisions.

We support decisions such as

  • Can the business afford additional employees?
  • Should you expand into a new location?
  • What working capital is required for growth?
  • Is a new product or service financially viable?
  • What information is required for banks or investors?
Outcome: Decisions supported by financial analysis rather than assumptions alone.
