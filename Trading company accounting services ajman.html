What are trading company accounting services in Ajman?

Trading company accounting services in Ajman cover bookkeeping, inventory and cost of goods sold, trade receivables and payables, multi-currency transactions, VAT records and financial reporting for companies that buy and sell goods, including import, export, wholesale and distribution businesses.

What does accounting for a trading company involve?

It involves recording purchases and sales, tracking stock and landed cost, calculating cost of goods sold and gross margin, managing customer and supplier balances, handling foreign currency, and keeping VAT and customs-related records organised.

How do you handle inventory and cost of goods sold?

We record purchases, stock movements and import costs such as freight and duties, then calculate cost of goods sold and gross margin so you can see which products and customers are profitable.

What is landed cost and why does it matter for trading companies?

Landed cost is the full cost of getting goods into your stock: the supplier price plus freight, insurance, customs duty, clearing charges and handling. Using landed cost instead of the invoice price alone gives a true cost of goods sold and a reliable gross margin for each product.

Can you manage import, export and multi-currency transactions?

Yes. We record foreign currency purchases and sales, track exchange differences and keep supporting records for imports and exports organised so reconciliation and reporting are easier.

Do trading companies in the UAE need to register for VAT and corporate tax?

UAE VAT registration is mandatory when taxable supplies and imports exceed AED 375,000 over the previous 12 months or are expected to in the next 30 days, and the standard rate is 5%. Corporate tax is 9% on taxable income above AED 375,000, and most businesses, including many free zone companies, need to register. Check the Federal Tax Authority for your situation.

How often do trading companies file VAT returns in the UAE?

VAT returns are generally filed quarterly, and the Federal Tax Authority can assign some businesses a monthly tax period. Returns are normally due within 28 days after the end of the tax period. Confirm your assigned period and deadlines on your FTA account.

Can you support trading companies in Ajman on the mainland and in free zones?

Yes. We support general trading, import-export, wholesale and distribution companies in Ajman, on the mainland or in free zones such as Ajman Free Zone. VAT and corporate tax treatment depend on your licence and activity, so we review your setup against Federal Tax Authority guidance.

Do free zone trading companies in Ajman pay 0% corporate tax?

Not automatically. A free zone company may qualify for a 0% rate only on qualifying income and only if it meets the conditions to be a Qualifying Free Zone Person. Other income is generally taxed at 9% above AED 375,000. Eligibility depends on your activity, customers and substance, so review it against Federal Tax Authority guidance.

How long must a trading company keep accounting records in the UAE?

VAT records must generally be kept for five years, and corporate tax records for seven years. Keep invoices, bank statements, import and export documents and ledgers organised so they can be produced for a review.

What documents do you need to get started?

Typically the trade licence, sales and purchase invoices, bank statements, stock lists, import and export documents such as bills of lading and customs declarations, previous VAT returns and any existing ledgers. We tell you exactly what is missing after the first review.
