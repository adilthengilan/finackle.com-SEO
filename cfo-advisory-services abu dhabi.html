Is it time?

When does your Abu Dhabi business need CFO support?

Having an accountant and maintaining accurate books are essential. But reliable historical accounts do not always tell you what to do next.

You may need additional CFO support if:

1

Cash flow is unpredictable

You need to understand upcoming receipts, supplier payments, payroll and other commitments before cash becomes tight.

2

You are growing but cannot see your true margins

Revenue is increasing, but you are unsure which products, services, projects or customers are contributing most to profit.

3

Financial reports do not lead to clear decisions

You receive a profit and loss statement but lack commentary explaining what changed and why.

4

You are planning a significant investment

Hiring, expansion, new equipment or additional inventory needs to be assessed against your cash position and financial forecasts.

5

Your business has outgrown spreadsheets

Reporting takes too long, figures are inconsistent, and management spends more time assembling information than using it.

6

Banks, investors or partners need better financial information

You need structured forecasts, performance reporting or financial analysis to support discussions.

Finackle helps turn financial information into a practical management tool, so you can make decisions based on a more complete view of your business.

CFO advisory services

What is included in our CFO advisory services?

Our CFO support connects financial reporting with planning, analysis and decision-making. The exact scope is agreed around your business, the quality of your existing financial records and the level of support you require.

1

Cash Flow Forecasting and Working Capital

Profit and cash are not the same thing. A profitable business can still experience cash pressure when customers pay late, inventory builds up or major expenses fall due before receipts arrive. We help you develop a clearer view of expected cash movements, upcoming commitments and potential funding gaps.

Depending on the agreed scope, this can include

  • Rolling cash flow forecasts, including a 13-week forecast where appropriate
  • Tracking expected customer receipts and supplier payments
  • Reviewing receivables and working capital requirements
  • Assessing how planned expenditure could affect available cash
  • Identifying potential cash shortfalls early enough to consider practical responses
The outcome: Better visibility into when cash may become available, when it may be needed and which actions deserve attention.
2

Management Reporting and Financial Performance

Financial statements tell you what happened. Useful management reporting also helps explain why it happened and what it could mean for the business. We can help structure a monthly management pack around the information you need to run your business.

This may include

  • Profit and loss reporting with month-on-month comparisons
  • Balance sheet and cash flow information
  • Key performance indicators relevant to your business model
  • Budget-versus-actual analysis
  • Commentary on material movements in revenue, costs and margins
  • Clear priorities for management review
The outcome: Less time interpreting disconnected figures and more time addressing the issues that affect performance.
3

Budgeting and Financial Forecasting

A budget provides a financial plan. A forecast helps you assess how the business is tracking against that plan as conditions change. Finackle can help you develop budgets and financial forecasts based on expected revenue, operating costs, staffing, investment and other relevant business drivers.

Our support can include

  • Annual budgets and periodic reforecasting
  • Budget-versus-actual reviews
  • Revenue and expense assumptions
  • Scenario analysis for hiring, expansion or investment
  • Assessing the financial implications of changing business plans
The outcome: A clearer understanding of what your plans require financially and how different decisions may affect the business.
4

Profitability, Pricing and Margin Analysis

Increasing sales does not necessarily mean increasing profit. Discounts, operating costs, supplier prices and delivery or platform fees can all affect the contribution your business earns. CFO advisory can help you examine the financial drivers behind profitability.

Depending on your business and the available data, this may include

  • Reviewing gross and operating margins
  • Analysing profitability by product, service or sales channel
  • Reviewing direct costs and overheads
  • Assessing pricing assumptions and cost changes
  • Identifying areas that may require further investigation
The outcome: Better information for pricing, cost control and decisions about where to focus your resources.
5

Financial Planning for Major Business Decisions

Business owners often need to make decisions before every outcome is certain. A structured financial assessment can help clarify the assumptions, risks and potential consequences.

We can support decisions such as

  • Whether the business can afford additional employees
  • Whether expansion fits the current financial position
  • How a new product or service could affect profitability
  • What level of working capital a growth plan may require
  • What financial information may be needed for bank, investor or board discussions
The outcome: Decisions supported by financial analysis rather than revenue figures or intuition alone.
Choosing a model

Virtual CFO vs Outsourced CFO: which is right for your business?

The terms overlap, and providers use them differently. What matters most is the actual responsibility, availability and deliverables included in your engagement.

Virtual CFO

A virtual CFO typically provides senior financial guidance remotely or on a part-time basis. This model may suit a business that has accounting processes in place but needs help with forecasting, management reporting, budgets and strategic financial decisions.

Outsourced CFO

An outsourced CFO arrangement can cover a broader or more continuous set of finance responsibilities, depending on the agreed scope. It may include regular reporting, forecasting, financial reviews and coordination with your existing accounting team. This may suit a business that needs ongoing external finance leadership without recruiting a full-time CFO.

In-house CFO

An in-house CFO is an employee who can provide dedicated leadership and work closely with management on an ongoing basis. This may be appropriate when the scale, complexity or demands of the business justify a full-time senior finance role.

How Finackle can help: We discuss your current finance setup, the decisions you need support with and the reporting you expect. From there, we can recommend an appropriate scope rather than assuming every business needs the same arrangement.

CFO support for businesses operating in Abu Dhabi

Built around how your business earns, spends and collects.

Businesses operating in Abu Dhabi may have different financial planning requirements depending on their sector, legal structure, customer contracts and growth plans. The useful starting point is to understand how your business earns, spends and collects money.

Mainland and Free Zone Businesses

Whether your company operates on the Abu Dhabi mainland or through a free zone, your management reporting should reflect your actual operating structure and business needs.

For businesses with multiple entities, reporting can be structured to improve visibility across entities where the underlying records and agreed scope allow it.

ADGM, KEZAD and Masdar City Businesses

Companies operating through ADGM, KEZAD or Masdar City may have different operating models and reporting requirements. The relevant finance support depends on the business, its activities and its existing accounting arrangements.

Finackle can discuss how budgeting, cash flow planning, management reporting and financial oversight may fit your circumstances. Any specific jurisdictional compliance or reporting requirement should be confirmed against your company’s circumstances rather than assumed from its location alone.

Trading and Distribution Businesses

Inventory purchases, supplier credit, customer payment terms and stock turnover can all affect working capital.

Financial reporting can help you understand where cash is tied up, how costs affect margins and how purchasing decisions fit expected demand.

Professional Services Businesses

Service businesses often need better visibility into project economics, utilisation, billing and collections.

Depending on the available data, management reporting can help identify which services or engagements contribute most to financial performance.

E-commerce and F&B Businesses

Sales growth can conceal differences in profitability between channels, products or branches. Payment processing fees, refunds, delivery commissions, inventory and operating costs can all affect margins.

CFO support can help connect these operating drivers with the financial information used for planning and decision-making.

Monthly CFO reporting

What could your monthly CFO reporting look like?

The value of a finance review is not the number of charts in a report. It is whether the information helps you identify issues, understand performance and decide what to do next.

A management pack designed around your business could include the following:

Financial performance

  • Revenue and profit trends
  • Gross margin and operating costs
  • Comparison with budget and previous periods

Cash position

  • Available cash and expected movements
  • Upcoming payments and expected receipts
  • Forecast cash requirements and potential shortfalls

Business performance

  • Relevant KPIs for your industry and operating model
  • Product, service or channel profitability where the data supports it
  • Significant movements that require management attention

Decisions and priorities

  • Areas requiring further investigation
  • Financial implications of proposed changes
  • Recommended next steps for management discussion

Illustrative reports and dashboard figures are examples of how financial information may be presented. They are not representations of Finackle client results.

Frequently asked questions about CFO services in Abu Dhabi

What do CFO advisory services in Abu Dhabi include?

CFO advisory services can include cash flow forecasting, budgeting, financial performance analysis, management reporting, profitability reviews and support for major business decisions. The exact services depend on the provider and agreed engagement.

What is the difference between a CFO and an accountant?

An accountant generally focuses on maintaining financial records, preparing accounts and supporting compliance. A CFO focuses more on financial planning, performance analysis, forecasting and decision support. These roles complement one another, and reliable accounting information is an important foundation for CFO advisory.

Can I hire a virtual CFO without employing a full-time CFO?

Yes. A virtual CFO arrangement can provide access to financial planning and advisory support without creating a full-time CFO position. The level of involvement and deliverables should be agreed in advance.

Does Finackle support businesses in Abu Dhabi free zones?

Finackle provides finance and accounting services for UAE mainland and free zone businesses. CFO advisory requirements should be discussed individually to establish the appropriate scope for your company’s structure and operations.

Can an outsourced CFO help if my business is profitable but short of cash?

Yes. Cash flow forecasting can help compare expected receipts with upcoming payments and commitments. It can improve visibility into potential funding gaps, although it cannot guarantee that customers will pay on time or eliminate cash flow risk.

Do my books need to be up to date before I engage a CFO?

Not necessarily. If your records are incomplete or behind, an initial clean-up may be needed before reliable management reporting and forecasting can be produced. Finackle also provides accounting and bookkeeping support, subject to an agreed scope.

How much do CFO advisory services cost in Abu Dhabi?

Fees depend on the complexity of your business, the quality of your existing financial records, the reporting required and the level of ongoing involvement. Finackle can discuss the appropriate scope and provide a fee proposal based on your requirements.
