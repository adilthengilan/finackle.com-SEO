What is F&B finance support in the UAE?

F&B finance support in the UAE gives restaurants and food businesses finance-team capability without a full-time hire, across one outlet or many emirates. What is F&B finance support in the UAE?

F&B finance support in the UAE gives restaurants, cafes, cloud kitchens and food businesses finance-team capability without a full-time hire. It covers food cost analysis, cash flow management, budgeting and forecasting, management reporting and profitability by outlet, menu category and sales channel.

Do you serve F&B businesses in every emirate?

Yes. We support F&B businesses across the UAE, including Abu Dhabi, Dubai, Ajman, Fujairah, Umm Al Quwain and Ras Al Khaimah.

Can you consolidate reporting for several outlets and entities?

Yes. We build one reporting structure so owners see group and outlet performance together.

Can you support a franchise operator?

Yes. We include franchise fees and marketing contributions in outlet profit and cash planning.

Can you track central kitchen stock transfers?

Yes. We account for transfers between a central kitchen and outlets so food cost reflects reality.

Do restaurants and food businesses in the UAE need to register for VAT?

UAE VAT registration is mandatory when taxable supplies and imports exceed AED 375,000 over the previous 12 months or are expected to in the next 30 days. Voluntary registration is possible above AED 187,500. The standard rate is 5%. Check the Federal Tax Authority for current rules.
