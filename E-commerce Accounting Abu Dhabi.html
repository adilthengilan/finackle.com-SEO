UAE VAT for online sellers

UAE VAT quick facts
for e-commerce.

A short reference for online sellers. Rules can change, so confirm current requirements with the UAE Federal Tax Authority. General information, not tax advice.

UAE VAT essentials for e-commerce
Standard VAT rate5% on most taxable supplies in the UAE.
Mandatory registrationWhen taxable supplies and imports exceed AED 375,000 in the previous 12 months or are expected to in the next 30 days.
Voluntary registrationAvailable when taxable supplies or expenses exceed AED 187,500.
Records to keepTax invoices, credit notes, import documents, sales and purchase records, and marketplace or gateway settlement statements.
Why e-commerce is differentRefunds, marketplace fees, shipping and multi-channel sales all affect the VAT you charge and can reclaim.

What is e-commerce accounting in Abu Dhabi?

E-commerce accounting is bookkeeping and financial reporting designed for online sellers. It matches orders, refunds, marketplace settlements and payouts with bank receipts, and tracks inventory, fees and VAT so businesses in Abu Dhabi can see true profit.

Do e-commerce businesses in Abu Dhabi need to register for VAT?

UAE VAT is 5%. Registration is mandatory when taxable supplies and imports exceed AED 375,000 in 12 months, and voluntary above AED 187,500. Confirm current rules with the Federal Tax Authority.

Can you handle Shopify, Amazon, Noon and WooCommerce sales?

Yes. We reconcile sales from websites, marketplaces and payment gateways against refunds, fees and bank receipts.

Do you support Abu Dhabi sellers on Amazon and Noon?

Yes. We reconcile marketplace settlements, fees and refunds against bank receipts for sellers based in Abu Dhabi.

Can you handle cash-on-delivery reconciliation?

Yes. We match COD collections from couriers against orders and bank deposits, and flag unpaid, returned or short-remitted orders.

How much does e-commerce accounting cost?

Cost depends on order volume, number of channels, inventory complexity and whether VAT returns are included. After a short review we recommend a scope and quote.

What reports will I receive?

Typical monthly reports include a profit and loss statement, balance sheet, cash-flow view, VAT reconciliation and product or channel profitability analysis.
