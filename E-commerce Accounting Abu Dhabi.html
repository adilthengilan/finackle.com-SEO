What is e-commerce accounting in Abu Dhabi?

E-commerce accounting in Abu Dhabi is bookkeeping and financial reporting built for online sellers. It reconciles website, marketplace and payment gateway sales with bank receipts, tracks inventory and platform fees, supports VAT records and shows true profit by product and channel.

Do online businesses in Abu Dhabi need to register for VAT?

UAE VAT registration is mandatory when taxable supplies and imports exceed AED 375,000 over the previous 12 months or are expected to in the next 30 days. Voluntary registration is possible above AED 187,500. The standard VAT rate is 5%. Check the Federal Tax Authority for current rules.

Can you reconcile Shopify, Amazon and payment gateway payouts?

Yes. We match orders, refunds, fees and settlements from your website, marketplaces and payment gateways against the amounts actually received in your bank account, so your books reflect real revenue and costs.

How is e-commerce accounting different from regular bookkeeping?

E-commerce accounting handles high transaction volumes, multiple sales channels, refunds, platform commissions, shipping and inventory costing. Regular bookkeeping records transactions; e-commerce accounting also shows margins by product and channel.

Can you show which products and channels are actually profitable?

Yes. By combining product cost, shipping, payment fees, advertising and marketplace commissions with sales data, we report margins by product and by sales channel.
