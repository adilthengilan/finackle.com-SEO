What is e-commerce accounting in Abu Dhabi?

E-commerce accounting in Abu Dhabi is bookkeeping and financial reporting designed for online sellers. It reconciles website, marketplace and payment gateway sales with bank receipts, tracks inventory and platform fees, keeps VAT records in order and shows true profit by product and channel.

In the UAE, VAT registration is mandatory once taxable supplies and imports exceed AED 375,000, and the standard rate is 5%. See the UAE Federal Tax Authority's VAT guidance for current rules.

UAE VAT for online sellers

UAE VAT quick facts
for e-commerce businesses.

A short reference for Abu Dhabi online sellers. Rules can change, so always confirm current requirements with the Federal Tax Authority.

UAE VAT essentials for e-commerce businesses
Standard VAT rate5% on most taxable supplies in the UAE.
Mandatory registrationWhen taxable supplies and imports exceed AED 375,000 in the previous 12 months or are expected to in the next 30 days.
Voluntary registrationAvailable when taxable supplies or expenses exceed AED 187,500.
Records to keepTax invoices, credit notes, import documents, sales and purchase records, and marketplace or gateway settlement statements.
Why e-commerce is differentRefunds, marketplace fees, shipping and multi-channel sales all affect the VAT you charge and can reclaim, so settlements must reconcile to your VAT records.

Source: UAE Federal Tax Authority. General information only, not tax advice.

What is e-commerce accounting in Abu Dhabi?

E-commerce accounting in Abu Dhabi is bookkeeping and financial reporting built for online sellers. It reconciles website, marketplace and payment gateway sales with bank receipts, tracks inventory and platform fees, supports VAT records and shows true profit by product and channel.

Do online businesses in Abu Dhabi need to register for VAT?

UAE VAT registration is mandatory when taxable supplies and imports exceed AED 375,000 over the previous 12 months or are expected to in the next 30 days. Voluntary registration is possible above AED 187,500. The standard VAT rate is 5%. Check the Federal Tax Authority for current rules.

Can you reconcile Shopify, Amazon and payment gateway payouts?

Yes. We match orders, refunds, fees and settlements from your website, marketplaces and payment gateways against the amounts actually received in your bank account, so your books reflect real revenue and costs.

How is e-commerce accounting different from regular bookkeeping?

E-commerce accounting handles high transaction volumes, multiple sales channels, refunds, platform commissions, shipping and inventory costing. Regular bookkeeping records transactions; e-commerce accounting also shows margins by product and channel.

Can you show which products and channels are actually profitable?

Yes. By combining product cost, shipping, payment fees, advertising and marketplace commissions with sales data, we report margins by product and by sales channel.

What VAT records should an online seller in the UAE keep?

Keep tax invoices, credit notes, import documents, sales and purchase records by period, and marketplace or gateway settlement statements. Retention is generally five years, but confirm requirements with the Federal Tax Authority.

How much does e-commerce accounting cost in Abu Dhabi?

Cost depends on monthly order volume, number of sales channels, inventory complexity and whether VAT returns are included. After a short review we recommend a scope and quote.
