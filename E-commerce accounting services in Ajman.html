UAE tax context

VAT and tax for Ajman
online businesses.

Whether you sell from the Ajman Free Zone or on the mainland, your accounting records need to support UAE VAT and corporate tax requirements.

01

VAT Records

UAE VAT is 5%. Registration is generally mandatory above AED 375,000 of taxable supplies and voluntary above AED 187,500. We keep clean sales, purchase and refund records for your filings.

02

Corporate Tax Readiness

Corporate tax applies at 9% on taxable income above AED 375,000. Organised books and accurate margins make tax calculations simpler and avoid year-end surprises.

03

Free Zone & Mainland

Reporting structured around your licence type and selling model, including marketplaces, own websites and cross-border orders.

FAQ

E-commerce accounting in Ajman:
common questions.

Quick answers to what online business owners in Ajman ask most about e-commerce accounting.

What is e-commerce accounting?

E-commerce accounting is bookkeeping and financial reporting designed for online businesses. It records sales from your website and marketplaces, reconciles payment gateway and courier settlements with bank receipts, tracks inventory and cost of goods sold, supports VAT records and shows true profitability by product and channel.

How is e-commerce accounting different from regular bookkeeping?

Regular bookkeeping records transactions. E-commerce accounting also handles high order volumes, marketplace commissions, payment gateway fees, refunds, returns, cash on delivery settlements and inventory costing, so your reports reflect what you actually earned after all platform costs.

Do I need to register for VAT for my online store in the UAE?

UAE VAT registration is generally mandatory when taxable supplies exceed AED 375,000 over the previous 12 months or are expected to in the next 30 days, and voluntary above AED 187,500. Confirm your position with the UAE Federal Tax Authority or your tax adviser.

Can you reconcile marketplace, payment gateway and cash on delivery sales?

Yes. We match orders from platforms such as Shopify, WooCommerce, Amazon and Noon with payment gateway payouts, courier cash on delivery remittances and your bank statements, and flag refunds, fees and differences for review.

How do you calculate profit per product or sales channel?

We combine sales revenue with product cost, shipping, platform commissions, payment fees, returns and advertising spend, so you can see margin by product and by channel instead of looking at revenue alone.

Which accounting software do you work with?

We work with cloud accounting software commonly used by online businesses, such as Zoho Books, QuickBooks and Xero, and can advise on the right setup for your sales channels and reporting needs.
